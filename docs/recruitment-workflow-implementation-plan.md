## The journey to deliver

The everyday path is **Your goals → Find jobs → Select jobs → Check job details → Answer questions → Prepare applications → Review & send**. These are progress stages, not seven mandatory page visits. Each role has a stable identity and one clear current action. A returning owner resumes the useful decision directly; changing a search does not erase previous results, chosen roles, drafts, or attempts. For the proposed first path, report Muse Code CLI readiness and route public discovery and selected-job public checks to Contributor; reserve Standard for application preparation; do not require a Codex/OpenRouter selector. Those are deferred alternatives. The stage indicator must mark runtime and Jev work; a shared collapsible activity view shows observable collection, checks and drafting without invented percentages or hidden-reasoning claims.

1. **Know me and my goals.** The app assembles reusable profile facts from owner-supplied material and keeps them distinct from desired next work. The owner can inspect and correct experience, search requirements and role criteria in context. The app owns the saved records; the owner is never required to fill a database-style form for facts the app can handle. A change is visibly saved and survives reload before the next search uses it.
2. **Find and assess real openings.** A primary **Find jobs** action uses the saved brief. The recruitment agent chooses sources, queries, public APIs and company career pages freely, using the app's bounded retrieval executor. Muse Code Contributor via the installed local CLI handles the full public vacancy-discovery loop, including choosing sources and general role/region/skill queries, collecting public vacancies and saving public evidence. Those generalized queries contain no private owner facts. Jev handles personalized classification and saved-reason choices. If a catalog needs LLM authoring, Contributor writes general positive, negative and missing-information reason choices once per brief version from non-identifying role/region/skill criteria. Bind the actual private owner requirements locally and through Jev without sending them to Contributor or weakening them. Do not generate per-job or on-open prose; Standard is not part of discovery or screening. Jev classifies collected listings and selects supported saved reasons; the explanation opens without another model call. Results go to **Recommended**, **Could be recommended**, **Probably not recommended**, or **Not recommended**. **Unknown** is an exceptional, explained unusable-evidence state, never the default for one missing field. Missing details stay marked for later checking. **Find more jobs** and **Change my search** are ordinary, context-aware actions that retain saved work.
3. **Choose, then check.** The owner can choose any job in any group. Discovery and Jev classification do not check deeper, even for Recommended. Selection alone saves a choice; the explicit **Check chosen jobs** action starts deeper work only for those roles, using Contributor for public vacancy details and actual employer-question evidence without private owner context. It reuses evidence, reads the full vacancy and route, identifies relevant gaps, documents, and the employer's **actual** questions. It never invents employer questions. The chosen action remains accessible on long results lists.
4. **Answer, prepare, review.** During Check job details, Contributor retrieves actual public employer questions and prepares generic question categories without owner-private input. Answer questions consumes those saved questions: Jev chooses among owner-approved saved answers plus `no_fitting_answer`, and deterministic code prefills an editable box or leaves it blank for the owner to change. Answer makes zero Contributor, Standard or other generative calls. Prepare may draft unanswered **required** answers only from verified owner facts; optional blank answers may stay blank, and missing facts hold the relevant application. It assembles coherent materials, saves direct exact edits without a model call, and uses Standard only in Prepare to draft grounded required answers and coherent CV/message materials, or on an explicit rewrite request that creates a new reviewable version. The owner inspects the exact destination, channel, CV, message, answers and version before explicitly sending. The app records the actual attempt, outcome and exact attempted material; uncertainty is not called delivery and is not retried automatically.

The earlier [operations-first page map](../../design/frontend/01-page-overview/README.md) puts sent history and replies in Applications, with interviews, offers and confirmed employment later in the service. The current seven-stage delivery covers finding work through immediate send outcome. It is **not** the whole recruitment lifecycle. Do not invent the deferred UX or restart the page map while fixing the first journey. The older proposal's automatic investigation when choosing a role is superseded by the explicit selected-job check decision above.
